Best Used Cars for South African Roads

Choose a used car for local commuting, long distances, gravel, family use or work.

Match the vehicle to the route

Consider ground clearance, tyre availability, fuel range, load capacity and comfort for the roads you use.

Running costs matter

Parts supply, servicing, insurance, tyres and fuel can matter more than a small difference in purchase price.

Buy on condition

A well-maintained example with records can be a better choice than a newer vehicle with uncertain history.
