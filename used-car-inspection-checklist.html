Used Car Inspection Checklist

Use this checklist when viewing and test-driving a used vehicle in South Africa.

Exterior and tyres

Check panel gaps, paint variation, glass, lights, tyre age and even wear.

Interior and controls

Test seatbelts, air conditioning, windows, locks, infotainment and every warning light.

Engine and road test

Start the car cold where possible; listen for noises and check steering, brakes, gearbox and temperature.

Documents

Verify VIN and engine details, licensing papers, service history, keys, warranty and written terms.
