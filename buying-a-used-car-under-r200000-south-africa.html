Buying a Used Car Under R200,000 in South Africa

Practical guide to choosing and checking a used car below R200,000, including running costs, finance and paperwork.

Set a complete budget

A R200,000 purchase-price ceiling is not the same as a R200,000 all-in budget. Allow for insurance, licence and registration costs, tyres, servicing, possible repairs and any finance charges.

Compare realistic alternatives

Shortlist vehicles based on passenger space, fuel use, safety equipment, service availability and everyday needs. Compare model year, mileage and documented maintenance rather than choosing solely by the lowest asking price.

Inspect before committing

Check tyres, brakes, lights, body panels, fluid leaks, electronics and dashboard warnings. Take a proper test drive and consider an independent inspection, especially if the vehicle has an incomplete history.

Check documents and finance

Verify the VIN against the vehicle and documentation, confirm ownership and outstanding finance, and ask for a written quotation showing the deposit, interest rate, term, fees and total amount payable. A low monthly instalment can conceal a balloon payment.
