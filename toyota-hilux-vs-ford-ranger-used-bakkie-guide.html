Toyota Hilux vs Ford Ranger: Which Used Bakkie?

Compare a used Toyota Hilux and Ford Ranger in South Africa: condition, maintenance, cab type, towing and total ownership costs.

Start with the specific vehicle, not just the badge

Both nameplates cover different generations, engines, transmissions and specifications. Compare vehicles of similar age, mileage, service history and condition before deciding. A well-maintained example is often preferable to a poorly maintained higher-specification one.

Engine, gearbox and service records

Confirm the exact engine and transmission, then check the manufacturer service schedule and evidence of completed work. Inspect for oil or coolant leaks, warning lights, unusual smoke and hard starting. Ask about major repairs and obtain an independent mechanical inspection.

Towing, payload and driving conditions

Check the exact model’s published towing and payload ratings, including restrictions and the weight of accessories. Consider whether the vehicle will be used mainly for commuting, gravel roads, towing or carrying tools. Never assume all Hilux or Ranger variants have the same limits.

Compare the total deal

Get insurance quotations, tyre and service estimates, and a written breakdown of finance and fees. Check tyres, suspension, 4x4 engagement where fitted, accident repairs and the VIN before signing.
