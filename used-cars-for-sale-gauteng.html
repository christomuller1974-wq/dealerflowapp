Choosing a used car in Gauteng

Daily commuting, busy highways and varied budgets mean buyers commonly compare automatic hatchbacks, economical sedans, seven-seat SUVs and versatile bakkies. With many dealer areas close enough for a day trip, searching beyond one suburb can unlock considerably more choice.

Explore stock across Johannesburg, Pretoria, Centurion and Midrand, as well as the East Rand, West Rand and Vaal. Compare the full advertised price, mileage, model year and dealer location before arranging a viewing.

Before committing, confirm that the vehicle is still available and ask about its service history, accident history, warranty position and any fees. Inspect it in person, take a proper test drive and verify the VIN and paperwork. If you need finance or have a trade-in, request a written quotation so you can compare the full deal rather than only the monthly instalment.

Build a shortlist before you travel. Compare vehicles of a similar year and specification, then note meaningful differences in mileage, safety features, transmission, fuel type and maintenance record. An unusually low price deserves careful checking, while a slightly higher-priced vehicle may offer better value if it has documented servicing, useful extras or warranty cover. Never send money merely to secure an unseen vehicle; confirm the dealership, inspect the car and make sure every promise that matters to you appears in the written agreement.
