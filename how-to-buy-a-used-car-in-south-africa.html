How to Buy a Used Car in South Africa

A practical South African guide to choosing, inspecting, financing and buying a used car.

Set a realistic budget

Allow for insurance, licence fees, servicing, tyres and fuel in addition to the purchase price.

Research the vehicle

Compare model years, specifications, mileage, service history and typical asking prices.

Inspect and test drive

Check the body, tyres, fluids, warning lights, electronics and road behaviour. Use an independent inspection when appropriate.

Verify and conclude

Match the VIN to the paperwork, confirm ownership and finance status, and get every promise in writing.
