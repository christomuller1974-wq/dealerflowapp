Used Car Finance Guide South Africa

Understand deposits, interest, balloon payments and affordability before financing a used vehicle.

Compare the total cost

Ask for the interest rate, initiation and monthly fees, term, deposit and total amount repayable.

Know balloon-payment risk

A balloon lowers instalments but leaves a large final amount and usually increases total interest.

Prepare your application

Keep identity, address, income and bank documents ready and check the quotation before accepting.
